# Training programs

## Overview

Mind Body Flow is a skill-based rotational training program led by Amit Kavale. It uses Indian Clubs, Mace, and Rope Flow to help participants move with more control, strength, rhythm, and intent.

The program is coach-led, progressive, skill-based, and designed for small batches of 5 to 12 participants. Each session is 1 hour. Equipment is provided by Mind Body Flow.

## The three tools

### Indian Clubs

Indian Clubs focus on movement and mobility. Controlled circular patterns develop shoulder mobility, scapular control, grip endurance, rhythm, and cross-body coordination.

### Mace

Mace training focuses on load and leverage. Asymmetrical loading teaches the body to manage grip, momentum, balance, stability, and rotational force under control.

### Rope Flow

Rope Flow focuses on rhythm and athleticism. Continuous movement develops footwork, timing, spinal rotation, spatial awareness, and multidirectional capacity.

## Program pathway

### Foundation course

The entry point for members who want to move better and build confidence with the tools. It covers tool orientation, mobility and posture, rhythm and timing, and safe progressions.

### Advanced course

For members ready to progress their practice through complex patterns, stronger rotational control, and greater movement variability. It covers layered combinations, rotational strength, conditioning blocks, and flow refinement.

### Integrated program

A regular practice blending all three tools. Participants should complete both the Foundation and Advanced courses first. It includes all three tools, ongoing practice, performance tracking, and active engagement.

## ROAR Simulation Protocol

The ROAR Simulation Protocol is a repeatable challenge inspired by the ROAR Games. It develops endurance, functional strength, rotational control, pacing, and mental resilience through a run, a Mace station, repetition, and a finish. The listed charge is INR 800 per head per session.

## Benefits

The program develops movement quality, rotational strength, coordination, force transfer, asymmetrical loading, acceleration and deceleration, and long-term engagement through measurable skill-based challenges.
